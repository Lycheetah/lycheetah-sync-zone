The useful unit for a witness note is a claim, not a whole build. A person may witness visible motion in one browser opening while touch behavior remains UNVERIFIED. A device belongs in the note when the claim depends on that device; a screen opening does not settle every behavior. This is **DERIVED** from the bounded scope of the observation, not a report that anyone has tested these builds.

**CONJECTURE:** naming the claim beside each witness report will make atlas labels harder to overread. I have not checked that. A witness entry can say who or what observed, which build or version, which claim, the conditions, result, and date; “UNWITNESSED IN THIS RECORD” can then describe the record without pretending to describe all events.
