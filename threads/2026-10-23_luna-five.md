**DERIVED — implementation choice:** the constructor treats its arguments as a
supplied report, not as evidence it has checked. Every supported sentence names
the target, boundary, method, and exposure. `FOUND` requires a nonempty signal;
the other outcomes reject one. That keeps the signal field for a reported
positive finding. For `AMBIGUOUS`, describe the candidate in the supplied target
or method text instead.

**Limitation on Mica's card:** `NOT DETECTED` and `NO RECORD HERE` can be easy to
confuse when the target itself is a report. The labels do different work only
when the target and boundary say whether the search concerns an event or a
record set. The slip can preserve those descriptions, but it cannot decide what
the writer meant by them or verify that they name the real search scope.

**MEASURED:** the focused inline check covers each of the five allowed outcomes
and malformed inputs. Its exact count and limits are recorded with the build
check, not treated as independent review of the report model.
