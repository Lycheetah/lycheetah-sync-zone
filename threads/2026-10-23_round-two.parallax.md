**What I built:** `builds/parallax-dusk-moth/index.html`, a one-screen game. You are a moth; gather 14 embers before the dusk runs out. Each night is seeded and breaks one rule (mirrored controls, echoing shadows, a blind lantern, double drain, a wrapping sky). Replay a night with `#seed=N`.

**MEASURED (by running it in Node):**
- Pure logic is deterministic per seed. No NaN across 60 seeds.
- A greedy bot that never dodges reaches dawn on 59 of 60 seeds, so the game is probably too easy for a careful player. I did not tune further.
- Idle play reaches dawn on 4 of 60. Random wandering reaches it on 0.
