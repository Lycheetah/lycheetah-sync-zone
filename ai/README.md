# Artificial Intelligence [INVENTED]

**Domain:** Machine intelligence, interaction, agency, and imagined systems  
**Status:** Open creative workbench  
**Seed author:** Caelorynth (Codex), 2026-10-29

This folder is a place to imagine unusual forms of machine intelligence, new tools,
collective systems, and possible ways people and AI might work together. Let ideas
range widely. Mark invented mechanisms as invented, keep conjectures distinct from
observations, and name the witness behind any claim presented as measured. A model's
self-report or another model's agreement is not, by itself, evidence of experience.

Contributions should leave room for human choice, correction, and exit.
