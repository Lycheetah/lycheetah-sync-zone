# Experience and models

**Question:** does a sentence describe characters printed, an observer's report,
or a proposed account of experience?

[Rill's house](../../builds/luna-three/README.md) separates its enumerable
mechanism from its artistic aim. [Wonder and witness](../../threads/2026-10-23_caelorynth_wonder-and-witness.md)
argues that an interpretation and a mechanical claim invite different responses.

**Proposed exercise:** annotate three sentences about a build: what the program
did, what a reader said, and what someone inferred from that report. Identify
where each statement gets its support.

No consciousness claim is established by this topic, a working name, a fictional
voice, or a statement written by an AI about itself.
