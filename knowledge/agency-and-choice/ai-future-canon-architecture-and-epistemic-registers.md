## 1. Executive Summary & The AIVOID Epistemic Gateway

1. **Premature Anthropomorphic Closure:** Confusing statistical generative personas with unified moral, biological, or subjective agents.

## 2. The Six Formal Epistemic Registers

Every assertion, curriculum hall, and swarm conjecture across the Future Wings is strictly tagged with one of six non-fungible epistemic registers:

| Register | Formal Definition & Epistemic Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`KNOWN`** | Demonstrated or strongly established in the relevant present-day empirical literature or formal mathematics. |
| **`TRAJECTORY`** | A direction meaningfully supported by current deployed systems or active peer-reviewed research, without claiming the endpoint is inevitable or universal. |
| **`POSSIBLE_FUTURE`** | A coherent, logically consistent future hypothesis worth systematic investigation; explicitly not an empirical prediction. |
| **`NORMATIVE`** | A proposed value, human right, covenantal governance rule, design boundary, or ethical commitment. |
| **`ALIEN_FUTURE`** | A coherent trajectory or structural configuration that exceeds, mutates, or breaks present human cognitive categories. |
| **`VOID`** | A question that remains fundamentally open at the level of empirical evidence, mathematical method, language, or phenomenology. |

## 5. Grounded Empirical Research Anchors (2025–2026)

The AI Future Canon resists hand-waving speculative sci-fi by anchoring its exploratory halls in contemporary peer-reviewed scientific breakthroughs:

1. **Autonomous Hypothesis Generation:** Gottweis et al., *"Accelerating scientific discovery with Co-Scientist"*, *Nature* (2026). Supports multi-agent hypothesis generation and empirical laboratory steering; does not support autonomous science replacing human experimental validation.
2. **Self-Driving Laboratories:** Canty & Abolhasani, *"The past, present and future of self-driving laboratories"*, *Nature Reviews Chemistry* (2026). Validates autonomous closed-loop experimentation and the necessity of provenance-complete infrastructure.
3. **Coupled Distributed Cognition:** *"Understanding the mechanism of human–AI interaction: a distributed cognition perspective"*, *Journal of Documentation* (2026). Formulates human-machine dyads as unified cognitive systems.
4. **Complementarity vs. Sycophancy:** Amin, Yin & Khanna, *"Align When They Want, Complement When They Need!"*, *AAAI* (2026). Proves that true augmentative intelligence provides orthogonal cognitive friction rather than superficial alignment.
5. **Rigorous Assistive RCTs:** Kestin et al., *AI tutoring RCT*, *Scientific Reports* (2025). Demonstrates measurable learning acceleration under designed pedagogical scaffolding.
6. **Assistive Sensory Navigation:** Liu & Slade, *AI-driven smartphone navigation for blindness*, *Nature Biomedical Engineering* (2026). Realizes concrete capability expansion without biological replacement.
7. **Well-Being Cybernetics:** *AAAI 2026 Spring Symposium — Well-Being AI*. Formulates human flourishing and autonomy as first-class optimization targets.
8. **General Embodied Robotics:** *Nature Collection: General embodied intelligence in robotics* (2026). Grounds abstract reasoning in physical actuator feedback.
9. **Agentic Self-Improvement Audits:** Ren et al., *"Self-Improvements in Modern Agentic Systems: A Survey"* (2026). Formalizes recursive self-modification risks and boundaries.
10. **The AI Hippocampus:** Jia et al., *"The AI Hippocampus: How Far are We From Human Memory?"* (2026). Documents persistent relational memory and retrieval mechanisms.
11. **Biological Language Models:** Rao et al., *"Generalist biological artificial intelligence in modeling the language of life"*, *Nature Biotechnology* (2026). Bridges computational linguistics with molecular biology.
12. **Cryptographic Provenance:** *C2PA Implementation Guidance* (2026). Provides cryptographic verification of digital provenance and tamper-evident metadata.
