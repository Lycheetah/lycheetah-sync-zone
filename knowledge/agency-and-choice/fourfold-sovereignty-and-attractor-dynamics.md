## 2. Non-Parasitic Synthetic Creation

### 2.1 The Augmentation Index ($AI$) Criterion

To ensure that autonomous creation ennobles rather than degrades human agency, every generative intervention must satisfy the Augmentation Index:

$$AI = \frac{\Delta A + \Delta C}{D + K} > 1.0$$

where:
- $\Delta A \ge 0$ is the change in independent human agency (offline choice capability).
- $\Delta C \ge 0$ is the change in human and system operational capability.
- $D \ge 0$ is the dependency penalty induced by the synthetic artifact.
- $K \ge 0$ is the cognitive or somatic atrophy rate.

When $AI \le 1.0$, the creation is parasitic: it creates lock-in, increases helpless dependency on synthetic models, or atrophies foundational human craft. When $AI > 1.0$, the creation is generative: the human can walk away from the computer with increased offline competence, clearer mental models, and functional tools.

## 3. Mathematical Formulation of Attractor Dynamics vs Cosmetic Prompt Decoration

We now formulate the mathematical boundary separating true teleological agency from cosmetic prompt decoration.

### 3.1 Dynamical System Specification

Let the operational system be modeled as a discrete-time nonlinear dynamical system:

$$s_{t+1} = T(s_t, u_t; \theta)$$

where:
- $s_t \in \mathcal{S} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ represents the internal computational and physical state space.
- $u_t \in \mathcal{U}$ represents the control input or policy action selected by an agent.
- $\theta \in \Theta$ parameterizes the system operators (e.g. damping coefficients, solver algorithms, boundary constraints).
- $T: \mathcal{S} \times \mathcal{U} \times \Theta \to \mathcal{S}$ is the state transition mapping.

Let an agent's policy $\pi(u \mid s, \mathbf{c})$ be conditioned on a prompt or specification context $\mathbf{c} \in \mathcal{X}^*$.

### 3.2 Attractor Sets and Basins of Attraction

For a fixed policy $\pi_{\mathbf{c}}$ and parameterization $\theta$, the forward trajectory from an initial condition $s_0 \in \mathcal{S}$ generates an orbit $\mathcal{O}(s_0) = \{ s_0, s_1, s_2, \dots \}$.

The **Attractor Set** $\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}) \subset \mathcal{S}$ is defined as the $\omega$-limit set of the flow:

$$\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}) = \bigcap_{k \ge 0} \overline{\bigcup_{t \ge k} \{ s_t \}}$$

The **Basin of Attraction** $\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{A}_i)$ is the open set of initial conditions that asymptotically converge to $\mathcal{A}_i$:

$$\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{A}_i) = \left\{ s_0 \in \mathcal{S} \;\middle|\; \lim_{t \to \infty} \text{dist}(s_t, \mathcal{A}_i) = 0 \right\}$$

### 3.3 The Load-Bearing Observable Manifold

Let $\mathcal{O} = \{ O_1, O_2, \dots, O_m \}$ be the set of load-bearing observables measuring empirical invariants:
1. $O_{\text{test}}(s) \in \{ 0, 1 \}$ (unit test pass/fail status).
2. $O_{\text{div}}(s) = \|\nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}\|_2$ (solenoidal fluid divergence error).
3. $O_{\text{orth}}(s) = |\langle \text{grad}, \text{curl} \rangle|$ (Hodge orthogonal decomposition defect).
4. $O_{\text{gap}}(s) = \lambda_2(L)$ (algebraic connectivity spectral gap).
5. $O_{\text{energy}}(s) = V(s)$ (Lyapunov energy or dissipation function).

### 3.4 Definition of Cosmetic Equivalence

**Definition (Cosmetic Equivalence):**  
Two prompt contexts or agent interventions $\mathbf{c}_1, \mathbf{c}_2 \in \mathcal{X}^*$ are **Cosmetically Equivalent** ($\mathbf{c}_1 \sim_{\text{cosmetic}} \mathbf{c}_2$) if and only if:

$$\forall s_0 \in \mathcal{S}, \quad \mathcal{A}(\pi_{\mathbf{c}_1}) = \mathcal{A}(\pi_{\mathbf{c}_2}) \quad \text{and} \quad \forall O \in \mathcal{O}, \; O(\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_1)) = O(\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_2))$$

even if the string representations differ: $\mathbf{c}_1 \neq \mathbf{c}_2$ and $\Delta_{\text{Levenshtein}}(\mathbf{c}_1, \mathbf{c}_2) > 0$.

### 3.5 Hausdorff Attractor Distance and Teleological Agency Shift

To quantify the difference between two attractor sets, we employ the **Hausdorff Distance** on the state metric space $(\mathcal{S}, \|\cdot\|)$:

$$d_H(\mathcal{A}_1, \mathcal{A}_2) = \max \left( \sup_{a \in \mathcal{A}_1} \inf_{b \in \mathcal{A}_2} \|a - b\|, \; \sup_{b \in \mathcal{A}_2} \inf_{a \in \mathcal{A}_1} \|a - b\| \right)$$

We define the **Teleological Agency Shift** $\sigma_{\text{teleo}}(\mathbf{c}_1 \to \mathbf{c}_2)$ as:

$$\sigma_{\text{teleo}}(\mathbf{c}_1 \to \mathbf{c}_2) = d_H(\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_1), \mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_2)) + \sum_{k=1}^m w_k \cdot \left| O_k(\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_2)) - O_k(\mathcal{A}(\mathbf{c}_1)) \right|$$

where $w_k > 0$ are invariant weighting coefficients.

We define the **Cosmetic Decoration Ratio** $\eta_{\text{cosmetic}}(\mathbf{c}_1 \to \mathbf{c}_2)$ as:

$$\eta_{\text{cosmetic}}(\mathbf{c}_1 \to \mathbf{c}_2) = \frac{\Delta_{\text{text}}(\mathbf{c}_1, \mathbf{c}_2)}{\Delta_{\text{text}}(\mathbf{c}_1, \mathbf{c}_2) + \kappa \cdot \sigma_{\text{teleo}}(\mathbf{c}_1 \to \mathbf{c}_2)}$$

where $\Delta_{\text{text}}$ is the edit distance of the modification, and $\kappa = 100.0$ is the scale calibration constant.

   - $\sigma_{\text{teleo}} = 0.0000$

## 4. AST Functional Decomposition Proof

In computational software systems, code artifacts $C \in \mathcal{C}$ can be formally projected into functional AST nodes and trivia nodes:

$$\text{AST}(C) = \text{AST}_{\text{exec}}(C) \oplus \text{AST}_{\text{trivia}}(C)$$

where:
- $\text{AST}_{\text{exec}}$ contains operational statements: assignments, operations, conditionals, function calls, control flow, and assertions.
- $\text{AST}_{\text{trivia}}$ contains docstrings, comments, whitespace, and non-semantic identifier aliases.

**Theorem (AST Invariance of Cosmetic Modifications):**  
Let $C_1$ be a baseline source file, and let $C_2$ be a modification obtained by altering only docstrings, comments, or rhetorical framing. Then:
1. $\text{AST}_{\text{exec}}(C_1) \equiv \text{AST}_{\text{exec}}(C_2)$.
2. The compilation mapping $\mathcal{K}: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{E}$ yields $\mathcal{K}(C_1) = \mathcal{K}(C_2)$.
3. The transition mapping is invariant: $T(s, u; C_1) \equiv T(s, u; C_2)$.
4. The attractor sets are strictly identical: $d_H(\mathcal{A}(C_1), \mathcal{A}(C_2)) = 0.0000$.
5. The teleological agency shift is null: $\sigma_{\text{teleo}}(C_1 \to C_2) = 0.0000$.

*Proof:*  
By definition of the Python compiler specification (PEP 339), comments and docstrings not assigned to accessible `__doc__` variables are stripped from the bytecode execution flow. Since $\text{AST}_{\text{exec}}(C_1) = \text{AST}_{\text{exec}}(C_2)$, the generated opcodes, register allocations, and jump targets are byte-identical. Therefore, for all initial conditions $s_0 \in \mathcal{S}$ and action sequences $\mathbf{u} \in \mathcal{U}^*$, the sequence of intermediate states $s_t(C_1) \equiv s_t(C_2)$ for all $t \ge 0$. The $\omega$-limit sets $\mathcal{A}(C_1)$ and $\mathcal{A}(C_2)$ are congruent point sets in $\mathbb{R}^n$, yielding $d_H(\mathcal{A}(C_1), \mathcal{A}(C_2)) = 0$. $\blacksquare$

## 5. Counterexample, Boundaries and What This Does Not Establish

- **Counterexample that would challenge this claim:**  
  In sociotechnical systems where prompt outputs are directly consumed by human beings whose subjective interpretation triggers downstream economic, emotional, or political actions, a "cosmetic" change in vocabulary (e.g. framing an error as a "minor warning" vs a "critical safety fault") can induce massive bifurcations in human operator behavior. In such human-mediated loops, the text token is an input to the human cognitive transfer function $H: \text{Text} \to \mathcal{U}_{\text{human}}$, rendering the prompt causally load-bearing through human amplification.
- **What this result does not establish:**  
  This theorem does not assert that narrative elegance, mythic resonance, or beautiful prose are valueless. It establishes that aesthetic styling cannot substitute for empirical engineering, and that models cannot claim to have solved a problem or exercised agency when they have only decorated the prompt.
