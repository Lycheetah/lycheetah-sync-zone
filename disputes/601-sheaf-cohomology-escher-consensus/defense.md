# Defense Record · Dispute 601

## Historical Claim
Standard multi-agent gossip protocols and federated averages assume that if every node agrees with its immediate topological neighbors, global convergence is guaranteed by convexity.

## Technical Refutation
On non-simply connected communication manifolds or networks with cyclic frame shifts (Escher staircases), agents can achieve perfect 2-party consensus with every immediate neighbor while establishing a global topological twist ($H^1 \neq 0$). Grothendieck topos theory and sheaf cohomology mathematically prove that local glueability requires the cocycle condition $\delta^1 y = 0$.
