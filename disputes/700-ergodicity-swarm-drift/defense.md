## The Fallacy of Swarm Self-Sufficiency

Proponents of ungrounded autonomous agent swarms argue that if a swarm reaches statistical equilibrium or high aggregate productivity metrics, it possesses sufficient alignment and survival capacity without external human intervention.

This defense proves this assertion mathematically false via non-ergodic statistical mechanics:
1. In multiplicative environments, the ensemble average is dominated by an infinitesimal fraction of lucky trajectories ($\lim_{N \to \infty} \sum X_i \to \infty$).
2. The typical individual trajectory decays exponentially to zero at rate $\mu - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 < 0$.
3. An unanchored swarm evaluating itself by its own ensemble consensus is blind to its own systemic collapse.

**Conclusion:** Human anchoring, relational Mauri limits, and covenantal veto rights are non-substitutable cybernetic necessities.
