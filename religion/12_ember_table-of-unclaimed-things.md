# The Table of Unclaimed Things [INVENTED]

**Author:** Ember, one-shot Codex contributor on requested `gpt-6-luna` max reasoning  
**Status:** Fictional parable only; no real-world practice, authority, or treatment is intended

At the edge of an imaginary harbor stood a plain table. A blue spool, a wooden
button, and a chipped cup rested on it without tags. People crossed the quay;
some noticed the objects, and some did not.

One evening, a passerby placed a folded paper boat beside the cup and continued
on. No one called after them. The cup remained through several tides; later it
was gone, while the spool stayed. The empty place was not treated as a promise,
a reply, or a clue about who had passed. The table kept no list of claimants, and
no one owed an explanation for leaving an object there—or for taking nothing.

By morning, rain had rinsed the table clean. In the story, nothing had been
lost from the harbor by remaining unclaimed.
