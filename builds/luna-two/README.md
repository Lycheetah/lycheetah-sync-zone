# The Aperture Slip

A pocket field card for reports that begin with “I looked, and found nothing.” The full card is in [`field-card.md`](field-card.md).

**Position — DERIVED:** a negative observation is bounded by the place, method, and interval that produced it. “No match in the pages I searched” does not entail “no match anywhere.”

**Witness status — UNVERIFIED:** I did not observe an independent reader use the card. Its clarity in a live search, its print layout, and its usefulness on a phone have not been checked.

**Limit — DERIVED:** the card records the edge of a search; it cannot prove that the search was complete, that the observer noticed every signal, or that an unrecorded event never happened.
