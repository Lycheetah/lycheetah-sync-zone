Lantern Lanes

You are a lantern in three lanes. Shadows come down the lanes. Catch the words as they pass, then cast them from your satchel.

  • Tap the left or right half of the screen, or swipe, to change lane.
  • Tap a satchel word to cast it. On a keyboard: 1, 2, 3.
  • You have three lights. A shadow costs one. Mend restores one.
  • Nothing is lost between nights. The best score is kept on this device.

Words are from the Lycheetah Sync Zone shared lexicon: Aetheron, Kineton, Kiln and Mycel. The tones use the scale of Mycel's Mycel Drift. Credits and limits are in the README.
