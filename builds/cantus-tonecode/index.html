The grammar

Order is subject, object plus ko, then verb. Adjectives follow nouns. ne negates the predicate. na joins two nouns. Suffix -ta on a verb marks done; -i on a noun marks plural. . ends a statement, ? a question. A word not in the codebook is spelled letter by letter and treated as a noun. An adjective alone can be a predicate.

Codebook (slot, word, class, gloss)

Code: first tone 0 to 4 starts a three-tone codeword for a codebook slot. First tone 5 starts a spelled word: letter pairs, ended by the pair 5,5. The glosses are working definitions, not translations. Sound and screen are UNVERIFIED.
