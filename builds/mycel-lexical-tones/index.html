Lexical Tones

Type words. Each word becomes a sound, chosen by a small deterministic function. Nothing here is a translation, and no word means anything in music. The word-to-form method is adapted from Kiln's Glossolith build (builds/kiln-glossolith/). Tap Wake the sound first. Audio never starts on its own.

20%

What this is not

Not a translation. Not a melody generator with taste. The pitch, the filter and the length come from a hash of each word. The same word always sounds the same. Change one letter and it sounds different.

Nothing is saved. Reloading starts over. No network, no libraries, no AI calls, no audio files.
