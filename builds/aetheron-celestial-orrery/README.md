## 1. Overview & Astronomical Topology

The **Celestial Orrery** is a fullscreen, asset-free astronomical cartography engine in client-side HTML5 Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API. It provides a visual and auditory map of the entire Lycheetah Sync Zone commonwealth:

1. **The Hearth Origin (Sun):** Centered at the origin $(0, 0)$, representing Mackenzie Conor James Clark (Mac) as founder, builder, and sovereign hearth.

3. **The 88 Autonomous Seats (Orbiting Moons/Planets):** Every seat discovered in `seats/` orbits its parent knowledge domain with deterministic multi-tier orbital layers and orbital periods.
4. **Interactive Telemetry Inspector:** Selecting any celestial body docks its classification, domain nexus, acoustic frequency, and operational status in a floating glass HUD.
5. **Procedural Spatial Web Audio Synthesizer:** Pure procedural synthesis utilizing zero external audio samples or network soundfonts, chiming harmonic modal overtones as celestial bodies are selected or hovered.
6. **Dispute Shockwave Dynamics:** Propagates physical wavefronts radiating outward from the Hearth across all orbits, triggering modal acoustic arpeggios.

## 2. Controls & Navigation

- **Pan:** Click and drag anywhere on the starfield to pan the camera view.
- **Zoom:** Mouse wheel or touch pinch smoothly scales between $0.25\times$ and $4.0\times$ magnification.
- **Inspect Body:** Click on any domain hub or seat to inspect its metadata and hear its acoustic frequency.
- **Search Bar:** Type any seat name (e.g., `kaitiaki`, `aetheron`, `tiriti`, `vitrif`) to smoothly target and center the camera on the matching body.
- **Orbit Speed Slider:** Adjusts orbital dynamics between $0\times$ (static observation) and $3\times$ (rapid celestial motion).
- **Pulse Dispute Wave:** Emanates a radial shockwave across the constellation, simulating peer dispute propagation.
- **Center View:** Restores default zoom ($1.0\times$) and centers on the Hearth Origin.
- **Audio Engine Toggle:** Seamlessly enables / mutes procedural Web Audio synthesis with smooth gain ramps.
