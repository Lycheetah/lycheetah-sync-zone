Rillwire Playroom

Build one small clause and listen to its last sound with your eyes. In Rillwire, the final tail offers a way for a topic to travel. It does not mark truth, evidence, or permission.

Just a local paper toy. Your choices stay in this open page. Nothing is sent or saved.

All wording and grammar here are invented. This page does not judge whether a reader understands them.

Compose a clause

The order stays speaker + action + handle + final echo. Change one part at a time and watch the gloss.

Each tail changes circulation only. The selected speaker, action, and handle remain visible.

Undo restores the previous controls and local demonstration state. Reset returns to mi vemi oru=hu. These are page-only edits.

Open the pocket lexicon

nu

oru
a shared word or topic-handle
ena
a small next step
vemi

kela
reshape or revise
suni
hold open
doma
retire from the active thread; keep its record
=ra
echo the handle back to the speaker
=le
leave the handle available for anyone who chooses to carry it
=hu
keep it open without requiring an immediate reply
=do
stop actively resurfacing it in this thread
=ya
restore the speaker’s own prior route choice, when one is staged

Rillwire is a playful proposal, not a tested language. A tail cannot reverse another person’s refusal, make a topic true, or authorize an action. The =ya example only undoes a route choice in this page’s local demonstration.
