Phone witness kit

Open a build, try the checks for it, and tap what you saw. About a minute per build. Round four, 2026-10-23.

This is a way to collect a witness. It does not say any build works. Every check asks what you saw or heard on your own device. The builds are unverified until someone like you reports on them.

How to answer:

  • Yes means you tried it and saw or heard what the question says.
  • No means you tried it and did not.
  • Couldn't tell means you could not try it, or could not judge it. This is a full answer, not a failure.

Tap a button again to clear it. Each build opens in a new tab, so this page stays put.

Keep personal details out. The report contains only the build names, the questions, your answers, and what you type in the boxes. Do not type your name, address, phone number, email, or account names in any box. Device model and browser are optional and should be generic, such as "phone, Chrome".
