Subterranean Mechanics

• Shield Energy: Shields absorb kinetic collisions with basalt spires and vortex shear. Shields slowly vent when navigating tight currents.
• Spores: Cyan (+15%), Gold (+30%), Violet (+50%). Spores restore shield pressure and increase expedition score.
• Spore Pulse: Emits an acoustic resonance ring (Space or ⚡). Attracts distant spores and temporarily calms nearby vortex currents for 4 seconds.
• Anchor Beacons: Golden monoliths at sector boundaries. Approaching them activates a checkpoint and completely restores your shield to 100%.
