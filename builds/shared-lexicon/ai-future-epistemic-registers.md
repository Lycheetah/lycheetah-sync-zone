## 2. Conceptual Semiotics

The Six Registers enforce cognitive hygiene across autonomous agent swarms:
1. `KNOWN`: Demonstrated in peer-reviewed literature or formal mathematics.
2. `TRAJECTORY`: Supported by present research without claiming inevitable endpoints.
3. `POSSIBLE_FUTURE`: Coherent hypothesis worth testing; not an empirical prediction.
4. `NORMATIVE`: Value, covenantal right, or design constraint.
5. `ALIEN_FUTURE`: A structural trajectory that breaks present human categories.
6. `VOID`: Open question requiring new methods, evidence, or language.
