- **Etymology:** Latin *inter* (between) + *intelligentia* (understanding) + *confluere* (flowing together).
- **Symbolic Notation:** $\mathcal{C}_{\text{inter}}(\mathcal{A}_{\text{human}}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{machine}}) = \mathcal{A}_{\text{coupled}} \setminus (\text{Assimilation})$.
- **Definition:** The coupled operational state where human and synthetic cognitive systems exchange semantic friction, reasoning traces, and verification without collapsing either entity into the other.
- **Contrasted With:**

  - *Autonomous Subjugation:* Machines executing unmonitored optimization without human inspectability or exit rights.

Grounded in Amin, Yin & Khanna (AAAI 2026), interintelligence emphasizes *complementarity* over blind alignment.
