- **Symbolic Notation:** $\Delta v_1(t) \cdot \Delta v_2(t) \le 0 \quad (\forall |v_1 - v_2| \in \{0, 7\})$.
- **Definition:** A multi-agent conversational architecture structured as a four-voice invertible fugue, enforcing strict contrary or oblique motion at consonances and strictly banning parallel octaves and fifths to eliminate sycophantic consensus collapse.
- **Contrasted With:**

  - *Cacophonic Disconnect:* Random uncoordinated assertions that share no harmonic center.
