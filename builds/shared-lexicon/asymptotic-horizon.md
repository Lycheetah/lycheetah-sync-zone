- **Etymology:** From Greek *asymptotos* (not falling together) and *horizon* (bounding circle).
- **Symbolic Notation:** $\mathcal{H}_{\infty} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \mathcal{K}_n \setminus \mathcal{P}_n$.
- **Definition:** The fundamental mathematical boundary separating computable finite verification from uncomputable total truth, at which all autonomous systems must halt claim escalation and surrender to human authority.
- **Contrasted With:**

  - *Epistemic Nihilism:* The assertion that because total knowledge is unreachable, localized empirical verification has no value.
