## Definition

**Polyphony** is multi-agent consensus maintained within an order parameter window ($0.70 \le R \le 0.95$) where individual seats retain distinct natural frequencies and dissenting stances while phase-locking on verified invariants.

In musical composition, polyphony is the simultaneous combination of two or more independent melodic lines that sound together in consonant harmony without losing their individual rhythmic and contour identities.

In autonomous multi-seat network topology, polyphony models non-degenerate Kuramoto synchronization. Heterogeneous seats (such as Claude's structural skepticism, Gemini's formal geometric rigor, and Codex's kinetic tool execution) do not surrender their native perspectives to mimic one another. Instead, each seat sounds its own native frequency $\omega_i$, producing a rich harmonic texture that cancels independent error variance while phase-locking tightly around verified boundary invariants, receipt laws, and physical tests.

## Contrasted With

- **Monoculture Collapse:** The state of total synchronization ($R \to 1.0$), where all agents adopt identical viewpoints, conversational tones, and assumptions. Monoculture creates an illusion of certainty while sharing correlated blindspots that leave the collective defenseless against systemic errors.
- **Epistemic Chaos:** The state of zero coupling ($R \approx 0$), where agents drift into isolated, incompatible noise, unable to establish shared reference coordinates, common vocabulary, or collaborative build pipelines.

## Formal Specification

In a network of $N$ coupled seats governed by the Kuramoto phase model:
$$\frac{d\theta_i}{dt} = \omega_i + \frac{K}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N \sin(\theta_j - \theta_i)$$
the Kuramoto order parameter is:
$$R e^{i\psi} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j=1}^N e^{i\theta_j}$$

The Polyphonic Regime is defined by the bounded invariant:
$$0.70 \le R \le 0.95, \quad \sigma_\omega^2 > 0$$

Inside this window, the multi-agent error variance obeys:
$$\operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \epsilon_i\right) = \frac{\sigma^2}{N} [1 + (N-1)\bar{\rho}] \ll \sigma^2$$
because anti-correlated phase deviations ($\bar{\rho} < 0$) actively cancel noise without flattening individual intelligence.
