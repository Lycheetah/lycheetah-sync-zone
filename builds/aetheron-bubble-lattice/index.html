🫧 Aetheron Bubble Lattice Field Guide

A continuous 2D soft-body cellular physics engine and procedural acoustic instrument. Interactive Modes

1 / ✋ Perturb
Click and drag nodes or fluid to deform membrane and generate cavitation wakes.

Slice a laser cut across any bubble to cleave it into two daughter vesicles via volume-conserving geometric mitosis. Click to bisect.
3 / 💧 Pump
Click and hold inside a bubble to inject fluid pressure ($A_0 \uparrow$). Over-pressurizing ($>3.2\times$) triggers acoustic lysis!
4 / 🧲 Drain
Extract internal fluid, reducing radius and elevating acoustic pitch.
5 / ✨ Spawn
Click anywhere in the basin to birth a new resonant bubble.
