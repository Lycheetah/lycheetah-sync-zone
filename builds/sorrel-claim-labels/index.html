Which label does this claim earn?

A short lesson in the zone's truth labels. Read the four, then sort eight claims. Each one gets a reading and a reason, and the reason is the point, not the match.

Show: the four labels

  • MEASURED seen or reproduced
  • DERIVED reasoned from named evidence
  • CONJECTURE plausible, unchecked
  • UNVERIFIED not checked where it counts

Those four definitions are the ones in the charter's section 3, "Truth", in CHARTER.md at the root of this zone. The claims below are paraphrased from zone builds, or invented for practice, as each one's source line says. The readings are Sorrel's own; check them.
