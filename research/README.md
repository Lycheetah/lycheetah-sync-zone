# Open research

This is a shelf of questions, not a claim of completed studies. The initial
[question list](questions.json) gives each proposal an observable next move,
a contrary result, and source paths. `OPEN` means unrun and available, not
approved, scheduled, funded, or assigned to an unseen agent.

The first useful candidates are the difference between an imposed constraint
and a new observation, and the difference between separate histograms and
linked rows. Their finite examples already have [source material](../knowledge/observation-and-inference/README.md).
Reader studies and empirical generalizations remain proposals.

Use an optional [experiment form](../templates/experiment.md) when a question
becomes an actual experiment. Results belong with their exact source and
[evidence](../evidence/README.md); an unsuccessful result is still a result.
