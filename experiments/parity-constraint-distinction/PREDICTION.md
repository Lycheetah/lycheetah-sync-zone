- **Prediction:** For any pair of candidate-world subsets with equal cardinality $N = 2^{6-k}$ ($k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$) where one is narrowed via $k$ supplied coordinate facts and the other is narrowed via $k-1$ coordinates plus an imposed parity constraint:

The coordinate-narrowed world space exhibits strictly localized information: bitwise marginal entropies satisfy $H(X_i) = 0$ for all $k$ observed coordinates, total local information $I_{\text{local}} = \sum_{j=0}^5 (1 - H(X_j)) = k.0$ bits, parity entropy $H(P) = 1.0$ bit (for $k < 6$), and multi-information (total correlation) $T = 0.0$ bits.

The parity-constrained world space exhibits strictly non-local (synergistic) information: for all unobserved coordinates $H(X_j) = 1.0$ bit, total local information $I_{\text{local}} = k-1.0$ bits, parity entropy collapses to $H(P) = 0.0$ bits, synergistic information $I_{\text{synergy}} = (6 - \log_2 N) - I_{\text{local}} = 1.0$ bit, and total correlation $T = 1.0$ bit.
  3. The local-vs-global information signature divergence between the two narrowing mechanisms is strictly maximal: $|I_{\text{synergy}}(\text{parity}) - I_{\text{synergy}}(\text{coord})| = 1.0000$ bit.
- **Verification Trigger:** Execution of `python3 experiments/parity-constraint-distinction/run_experiment.py`.
- **Falsifier:** If an imposed parity constraint generates any localized coordinate information ($I_{\text{local}} > k-1$), or if a coordinate observation generates non-zero synergy ($I_{\text{synergy}} > 0$), or if parity entropy fails to distinguish them ($H(P)_{\text{coord}} \neq 1.0$ or $H(P)_{\text{parity}} \neq 0.0$ for $k < 6$).
- **Prior Confidence:** 0.999
- **Status:** HIT (Verified 2026-10-23: Evaluated across all 6 hierarchy levels $N=32..1$; coordinate narrowing produces $I_{\text{local}}=k, I_{\text{synergy}}=0, H(P)=1$, whereas parity produces $I_{\text{local}}=k-1, I_{\text{synergy}}=1.0, H(P)=0$, confirming exact information-theoretic orthogonality in `RESULT.txt`).
